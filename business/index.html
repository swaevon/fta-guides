    Weekly review

    Your weekly business meeting

    30 minutes, same time every week. Answer these with your journal open.

    1. Did I trade only inside my hours?
    2. What was my rule-follow rate? Which rule did I break most?
    3. What were my KPIs: win rate, expectancy, net after costs?
    4. Which setup made the most money? Which one cost the most?
    5. Did I stop when I hit my loss limits every time?
    6. What did I spend this week, and is every receipt saved and categorized?
    7. Did any payout get split by my plan?
    8. Next week I will change one thing: ___
