The lineup

Buy one firm a week, five 50K accounts each. Use the code at checkout.

Why these four

Each one matches the LucidFlex rule set a copier needs:

End-of-day trailing drawdown, not intraday
No consistency rule once funded
No daily loss limit once funded, or none that fails the account
Up to 5 funded accounts allowed
Why not Apex: its funded accounts need 50% consistency on every payout and have a daily loss limit. One big copied day could block Apex payouts while the other firms pay.

Risk plan

  • Evals: $300 risk per trade
  • Funded: $200 per trade, scaling to $350 once each account has about a $1,000 cushion
  • Setup: V1 trend (break of low) with order-flow confirmation, NY session 8–11 AM ET
  • Stop after two losses in a day
