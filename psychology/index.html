    Cheat sheet

    Warning signs

    FOMO
    You're about to enter because price is moving, not because your setup is there.
    Revenge
    You just lost and you want it back right now.
    Tilt
    Fast clicking, bigger size, skipping your stop. You're done for the day.
    Overtrading
    You're past your trade limit or trading out of boredom.
    Hope
    Price passed where your idea was wrong and you're still in.
    Reset
    Stand up, walk away for 15 minutes, reread your plan.
    Stop
    Daily max loss hit, daily target hit, or trade limit hit. Close the platform.
