The lineup

Buy one firm a week, five 50K accounts each. Use the code at checkout.

Why these four

Each one matches the LucidFlex rule set a copier needs:

End-of-day trailing drawdown, not intraday
No consistency rule once funded
No daily loss limit once funded, or none that fails the account
Up to 5 funded accounts allowed
Why not Apex: its funded accounts need 50% consistency on every payout and have a daily loss limit. One big copied day could block Apex payouts while the other firms pay.

Passing the Lucid eval

200,000 simulated runs at a 45% win rate and 2R, one trade a day, with Lucid's end-of-day drawdown.

Default rule passes more often but takes about twice as many trades. The add-on is the fast lane: two wins on two different days can pass, but there's less room for losses.

A simulation is not a promise. It assumes your live trading matches these stats. Your results will differ, and you can lose the full cost of every eval.

Copier

  • How it runs: chart and place the trade on one lead account in TradingView. The copier sends it to every other account.
  • Lucid: free built-in copier for the first 5 accounts
  • Across firms: Traders Connect (about $25/month), PickMyTrade, CrossTrade, or Tradecopia (runs on Mac)
  • Test it first: run the copier on two accounts before adding the rest
  • The risk: every account passes or fails together. One bad stretch hits all of them at once.
