    Cheat sheet

    The labels

    HH
    Higher high: a swing high above the last swing high.
    HL
    Higher low: a swing low above the last swing low.
    LH
    Lower high: a swing high below the last swing high.
    LL
    Lower low: a swing low below the last swing low.
    BOS
    Break of structure: a candle body closes past the last swing. Wicks don't count.
    CHoCH
    Change of character: a break against the trend. Stop trading the old direction.
    A+
    Structure so clean and obvious that anyone would label it the same way, plus a pullback into a key level.
